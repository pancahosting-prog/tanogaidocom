ABOUT US
PT. TANOGAIDO FANTASI PRATAMA

Your Gateway to Japan.

PT. Tanogaido Fantasi Pratama is an Indonesian company specializing in Japan-related services, providing comprehensive solutions for individuals and businesses seeking travel, education, and various services connected to Japan.

Established in 2007, Tanogaido was founded with a vision to make Japan more accessible to Indonesian people. Through our growing range of services, we aim to simplify every aspect of experiencing, exploring, and connecting with Japan.

We understand that every customer has different needs, whether they wish to explore Japan's destinations, experience its culture, pursue educational opportunities, or access essential services. Therefore, we continuously develop integrated solutions designed to provide convenience, reliability, and a better customer experience.

Our commitment is to become a trusted partner for anyone looking to discover opportunities and experiences in Japan.

MILESTONES & GROWTH

Our Journey

Since 2007, Tanogaido has continuously developed its business in response to the growing interest of Indonesian people in Japan.

Starting with travel and tourism services, we have expanded our offerings to cover a broader range of Japan-related needs, including private tours, visa assistance, transportation, travel essentials, Japanese language education, ticket reservations, and shopping assistance.

Our journey reflects our commitment to innovation, service development, and building long-term relationships with our customers and business partners.

Today, we continue to strengthen our position as an integrated Japan services provider, connecting people with the experiences, opportunities, and services they need.
