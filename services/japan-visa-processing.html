Japan Visa Processing — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book with our bilingual Japan concierge desk.

How long does Japan visa processing usually take?

Standard embassy/JVAC processing takes around 5 to 7 working days after filing, depending on seasonal volume.

Can you process Japan Visa Waiver for e-passports?

Yes, we handle e-passport Visa Waiver registration quickly and cleanly before your departure.

Who qualifies for a Japan Multiple Entry visa?

Frequent travelers with qualifying financial capacity or previous G7/Japan travel history may apply; we assess your eligibility upfront.

Do I need to buy flight tickets before applying?

We advise on compliant flight/hotel booking documentation so you do not risk non-refundable fares.
