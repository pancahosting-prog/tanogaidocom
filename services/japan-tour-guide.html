Why Book a Dedicated On-Site Japan Tour Guide?

Traveling across Japan is infinitely richer when you have an experienced local guide beside you. Whether you are navigating multi-level Tokyo subway interchanges, visiting historic shrines in Kyoto, or enjoying day trips to Lake Kawaguchiko and Hakone, your Tanogaido guide adapts the day around your rhythm and interests.

  • Full-day licensed bilingual guide (up to 10 hours per day)
  • Pre-trip itinerary calibration across Tokyo, Kansai, or Hokkaido
  • Live train route navigation, IC card setup, and luggage forwarding tips
  • On-the-spot translation for dining, shopping, and attraction counters
  • Direct WhatsApp coordination with Tanogaido operations desk

Japan Tour Guide — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book with our bilingual Japan concierge desk.

Which cities are covered by Tanogaido Japan tour guides?

Our licensed local guides operate across Tokyo, Yokohama, Mt. Fuji, Hakone, Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, Kobe, Sapporo, and Fukuoka.

What languages do Tanogaido tour guides speak?

All guides speak fluent Japanese alongside English and/or Indonesian to ensure seamless communication everywhere.

Can the guide help us use JR Pass and Shinkansen trains?

Yes. Your guide helps exchange vouchers, reserve Shinkansen seats, and escort you through station platforms.

Are guide transport and attraction fees included?

Guide service covers daily professional guiding fees; optional vehicle charter, rail passes, and entry tickets can be bundled cleanly on request.
