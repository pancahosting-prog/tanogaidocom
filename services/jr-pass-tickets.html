JR Pass Tickets — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book with our bilingual Japan concierge desk.

Is the nationwide 7-day JR Pass still worth it for Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka?

For a simple Tokyo–Osaka round-trip alone, single Shinkansen tickets are usually cheaper; adding Hiroshima, Kanazawa, or Kyushu makes passes shine. We calculate it free for you.

Can you issue tickets for the fast Nozomi Shinkansen?

Yes, we book direct Nozomi point-to-point tickets as well as Nozomi upgrade supplements if needed.

What is the difference between Ordinary and Green Car?

Green Car offers wider 2+2 seating, extra legroom, quieter cabins, and attendant service similar to business class.
