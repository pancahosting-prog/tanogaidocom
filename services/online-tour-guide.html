Online Japan Tour Guide — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book with our bilingual Japan concierge desk.

How does the Online Japan Tour Guide work during my trip?

You receive a dedicated WhatsApp concierge thread staffed by bilingual Japan specialists who reply within minutes to questions, photos, or calls.

Can you call Japanese restaurants or hotels for us?

Yes. We handle live phone reservations, dietary inquiries, and luggage coordination directly in Japanese.

Is Pocket WiFi needed to use the online guide?

Reliable mobile data is essential; we recommend pairing this service with our Japan Pocket WiFi Rental for uninterrupted coverage.

Can I upgrade to an on-site guide on specific days?

Absolutely. Many guests combine online guiding in Tokyo with an on-site guide for Kyoto or Mt. Fuji day trips.
