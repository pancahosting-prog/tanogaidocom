Japan Prewedding Packages — Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. We strictly follow all municipal rules and shoot exclusively at authorized public temples, parks, Arashiyama groves, and scenic Kyoto streets.

What happens if it rains on our shoot day?

We monitor forecasts closely and offer flexible backup date shifts or covered traditional wooden architecture locations.

Preview selects arrive within days, and your full color-graded gallery is delivered within 3 to 4 weeks.
