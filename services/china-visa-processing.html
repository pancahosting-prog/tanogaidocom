China Visa Processing — Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book with our bilingual Japan concierge desk.

Do all applicants need to appear for fingerprints?

Fingerprint requirements depend on current embassy exemptions (e.g., certain single/double-entry short stays or age brackets); we confirm exact rules for your case.

Can you assist with business (M) visas for trade fairs?

Yes, we verify your Chinese host company invitation letter and align your business itinerary.

How early should I apply for a China visa?

We recommend starting 3 to 4 weeks prior to departure to allow comfortable appointment and processing lead time.
